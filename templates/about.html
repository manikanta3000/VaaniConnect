About VaaniConnect

Our Mission

VaaniConnect is a multilingual conversational AI platform designed to break language barriers in South India. Supporting languages like Telugu, Tamil, Kannada, Malayalam, Hindi, and English, it enables real-time voice-to-text, text-to-speech, and translation for improved communication in education, healthcare, and local governance sectors.
